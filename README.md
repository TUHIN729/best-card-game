**Emberfall — Duel of the Three Winds** is an original browser card game: you duel Zephyr, the Gale Warden, with a hand of five spirit cards drawn from a 24-card deck in three suits — Flame, Tide and Gale. Every round both duelists reveal one spirit at once; the stronger power wins the round and deals the difference as damage. The suits form a cycle (Flame beats Gale, Gale beats Tide, Tide beats Flame, +2 power), playing the same suit twice in a row triggers a Surge (+2), and cards cost ember, so every hand is a decision about tempo, counters and resources. Spirits carry abilities — heal, draw, pierce — and Zephyr reads your last suit and answers it, spends big when he is behind, and hoards his heavy spirits while he is ahead. First duelist to zero life loses. It plays with mouse and keyboard (1–5 pick a card, Enter reveals, M mutes, R restarts), runs entirely offline from static files, and has original art, sounds and names throughout.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
